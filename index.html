Shorts Autopilot

Shorts Autopilot is a personal publishing tool used by Ray Kreisel to schedule his own short travel videos on his own YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook Page.

It uploads videos that Ray made, sets their titles, descriptions, tags, playlists and publish dates, and publishes them on schedule. It is not offered to anyone else, and it acts only on accounts Ray owns.

YouTube API Services

Shorts Autopilot uses YouTube API Services to upload and schedule videos on Ray's YouTube channel. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
